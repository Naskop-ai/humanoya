The €615 billion that sleeps

Almost every French household owns a Livret A. Very few know what else money could do.
12 October 2026 · 5 min read

A very large pillow

At the end of 2025, savers held about €615 billion on Livret A and LDDS accounts (Caisse des Dépôts, January 2026). It is the most trusted place to keep money in France, and for good reason: it is simple, it is guaranteed and it is available.

But trust and habit are not the same thing as a decision. Ask most people why their money is there and the answer is "because that is where it goes".

What the money could be doing

No one should empty a Livret A. The cushion is the foundation of every plan, as lesson 1 of our course shows. The question is about what lies beyond the cushion: the part that will not be needed for five, ten or twenty years.

For that part, an investment in a broad, low-cost fund has historically offered a higher expected return than a regulated savings account, with the price of movement along the way. The Banque de France measured the average financial culture of French adults at 12.45 out of 20, 14th among 39 OECD countries. The gap is not courage; it is explanation.

Who explains it to the people who need it?

The people who hold most of the sleeping savings are often older, and the people who are most curious about investing are often young. They live in the same families and rarely talk about it.

That is the idea behind Humanoya. A young person learns the basics in three hours, then explains them to an elder who brings what a course cannot: experience of crises, of prudence and of what money is for. Nobody is asked to invest anything on day one. The first step is a conversation.
