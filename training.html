The six lessons

  1. Saving or investing?

    Know when to keep money safe and when to let it work.
    Tell saving from investing. Build a safety cushion first. See what inflation does to idle cash.

    10 min

  2. ETFs and fees

    Own a thousand companies in one purchase and keep the fees low.
    Explain what an ETF is. Read the fee figures that matter. See why a small fee becomes a big gap.

    10 min

  3. Risk and time

    Understand falls, stay calm and let time work.
    Describe risk as movement, not loss. Explain why time reduces the odds of a bad result. Use regular investing to avoid timing the market.

    10 min

  4. Taxes, the basics

    Know the main account types and when time helps your taxes.
    Name the main account types. Explain why holding time can lower tax. Know when to ask a professional.

    10 min

  5. Scams to avoid

    Spot the traps that target savers, and teach your elder to spot them too.
    Recognise the five classic scams. Run a three-step check before any payment. Protect a relative.

    10 min

  6. The green option

    Invest in line with your values without falling for the label.
    Explain what sustainable funds are. Spot greenwashing. Weigh impact and return honestly.

    10 min
