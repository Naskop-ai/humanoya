Why we started

Our grandparents have money that sleeps. We have questions nobody answered.

Young people say they want to understand investing and rarely get a place to learn it. Older people hold much of the savings, and are the favourite targets of financial scams. They live in the same families and rarely talk about it.

Humanoya was born in a hackathon with Crédit Mutuel Alliance Fédérale in Reims. We kept one idea: give them a reason to sit at the same table.
