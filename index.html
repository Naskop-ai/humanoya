Humanoya in two minutesEnglish · captions on · voice-over

Elders' savings sleep in bank accounts. Businesses and projects lack funding. And young people want to invest, but nobody taught them how.

Humanoya brings them together. Learn online, for free. Mentor, with an elder. Invest, with an advisor. And fuel the real economy.

Meet Léa, twenty-two. She wants to invest, but does not know how. She discovers Humanoya, and signs up for free.

First she learns alone: six modules of ten minutes on saving, ETFs, risk, tax and scams. One badge for each.

Then she manages a practice portfolio with fictional money for four weeks, with weekly challenges. Real lessons, zero risk.

Then she invites her grandfather Jacques to be her mentor. He simply shares his experience: no training, and no money.

They meet at the Humanoya agency, sign the mentoring charter and choose the portfolio. The advisor reassures, and never sells.

Each week, over coffee or by video, Léa asks three questions. Jacques shares his reflexes, and together they decide one move.

At the final meeting, Léa presents her results and receives her certificate. Jacques is offered a free meeting with our advisor.

If he wishes, Jacques meets the Humanoya advisor to talk about his goals and his savings. It is free, with no obligation.

Then Léa opens her own Monabanq account, with a welcome bonus and matching. Jacques invests through a managed portfolio, with fees shown in euros. Their savings fund businesses.

The advisor follows Jacques every year, and new pairs join. Their savings keep fuelling the real economy.

Sleeping savings, a living economy. Training is free, and nobody pays before investing. Start your free course today.

Homonoia

The Greek goddess of concord. Our name joins her to the word humanitarian: sharing, including, passing on and protecting.

The symbolon

In ancient Greece two friends broke a token in two and each kept a half. When the halves fitted, the bond was proven. Here the young person keeps one half and the elder the other.
