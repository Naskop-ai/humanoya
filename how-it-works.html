The journey

Ten steps from your Monabanq account to confident, dynamic savings. You never pay to learn, and nobody pays before investing.

Start the free course

Open and learn

Steps 1 and 2. Open a Monabanq account, then learn at your own pace.

  • Open a Monabanq account and start learning: your personal space and practical modules open straight away.
  • Interactive modules of ten minutes: saving, ETFs and fees, risk and time, tax, scams, the green option.
  • A short film, a lesson and a quiz in every module, one badge each, with your progress saved.
  • Learn at your own pace: three hours in total, with the project and the final quiz.

Practise and certify

Steps 3 and 4. Practise risk-free, then earn your certificate.

  • A virtual portfolio of €10,000 across stocks, bonds and funds: watch it move, for example +2.5%, and press Start my simulation.
  • Zero euros at risk, with a challenge every week.
  • Pass the final quiz to earn the HUMANOYA certificate of completion in Financial Literacy & Informed Investing.
  • Watch two funds move side by side, one plain and one sustainable.

Meet a buddy

Steps 5 and 6. Exchange with a senior, then compare your goals.

  • Meet an intergenerational buddy: a senior who shares experience and gives personalised advice.
  • Family first: a grandparent, a parent, an uncle or aunt. No senior close by? A verified volunteer from our partner associations.
  • Compare your goals: buy a home in 1 to 3 years, save over 3 to 10 years, reach financial freedom beyond 10.
  • Your personal goals on one side, a shared perspective on the other. Your buddy does no training and sends no money.

Get guidance and grow

Steps 7 and 8. Meet the experts and track what you know.

  • Meet Humanoya and Monabanq experts: define your strategy and manage your risks. The advisor reassures and never sells.
  • Everything is booked in the app, in person or by video, with reminders.
  • Follow your progress: budgeting 100%, saving 80%, investing 60%, markets 40%.
  • Every module you finish moves a bar, so you always know what comes next.

See the impact, repeat

Steps 9 and 10. Understand your choices, then start the cycle again.

  • See the future value of your savings, the long-term opportunities, the risks and trade-offs, and the effect on your financial well-being.
  • Young people learn, seniors share, advisers guide, and choices become confident.
  • A new cycle starts every month, and sleeping savings are dynamised.
  • If you both wish to invest, fees are shown in euros and you can stop at any time.

You never pay

The bank pays us

When your Monabanq account stays active for six months, Monabanq pays Humanoya a commission. You pay nothing for the account or the course.

Elders pay for advice

Only if an elder chooses managed investing: an annual fee of about 0.6% of the assets we advise on, on top of the bank's management fee. Both are shown before signing.

No pressure, ever

Advisors are not paid per sale. Meetings are for learning first, and the training is yours whether or not anyone invests.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The training, the badges, the project and the certificate cost you nothing. Humanoya is paid later by the bank when an account stays active, and by a buddy only if they choose managed investing with our advisor.

Do I need a Monabanq account?

It is step 1 of the journey: a Monabanq account opens your personal space and the practical modules. The training itself is free, and your welcome bonus and matching apply once the account is active.

Who can be my elder?

A grandparent, a parent, an uncle or aunt, or any older person you trust. If no one is available, a verified volunteer from our partner associations and care homes can take the role. You can also do the course on your own.

Does my elder have to do anything online?

No. They do no training. They meet you, share their experience and give their opinion on the virtual portfolio.

Will anyone pressure us to invest?

No. The first meetings are about learning. If your elder wants to invest, a human advisor explains the options, with no obligation and fees shown in advance.

Can I lose money?

Not during the course: the project uses a virtual portfolio. If you later invest for real, the value can fall as well as rise, which lesson 3 explains in detail.
